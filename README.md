# ⚡ Electric Vehicle Market Performance & Insights

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an end-to-end market and performance analysis of Electric Vehicles (EVs) using Tableau Public, analyzing manufacturer volume, battery adoption, charging metrics, and environmental impact.

---

## 📊 Live Interactive Dashboard
- **Tableau Public Dashboard:** https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/rishi.gurjar/viz/EVMarketPerformanceDashboard_17931245740350/Dashboard1

---

## 🔑 Visualizations Included
1. **Sales by Manufacturer:** Volume analysis of leading EV manufacturers.
2. **Battery Breakdown:** Share of battery chemistries (LFP, NMC, NCA, Sodium-Ion).
3. **Battery vs Performance:** Acceleration vs battery capacity benchmarks.
4. **Global Production Map:** Geographic distribution across manufacturing countries.
5. **Yearly Sales Trend:** Annual sales trend lines across categories.
6. **Regional Sales Share:** Treemap of global market regions.
7. **CO2 Savings by Battery:** Total emission reductions achieved.
8. **Category Resale Value:** 3-year resale value percentage retention.
9. **Charging Time Dynamics:** Average charging duration across autonomous levels.
